IMO Dynasty V4.0.46 — Live H2H Odds Recalibration

- Championship Odds remain calculated from completed weeks; no changes to that model.
- Live H2H odds now incorporate actual live scores and the selected games remaining for both teams.
- If a team is trailing with no remaining players while the other team already leads, the display moves to $21.00 versus $1.05.
- If both teams are finished, the winner is strongly favoured; tied results remain neutral.
- Where live scores or reliable remaining counts are unavailable, H2H odds retain the previous projection-based fallback.
- Live odds remain indicative estimates, not actual bookmaker markets.
